Platform capabilities

Everything an event needs, nothing it doesn't

Built for academic institutions running seminars, workshops, and multi-day conferences with complex logistics.

📡
Live occupancy tracking

Watch headcounts update in real time via WebSocket. Every check-in pushes to the dashboard instantly - no refresh needed.

🤖
AI crowd forecasting

Predict expected attendance before your event starts. The model learns from registration patterns, venue, timing, and capacity.

📱
QR-based check-in

Participants download their unique QR code. Volunteers scan it on-site - fast, duplicate-proof, and fully auditable.

🏅
Auto certificates

Attendance certificates generate automatically once an event completes. Participants access them instantly from their dashboard.

👥
Volunteer management

Assign volunteers to specific events with designated duties. Each volunteer sees only their assignments and check-in logs.

📊
Event analytics

Per-event stats on registrations, check-ins, no-shows, and occupancy utilization - all in one place for post-event review.
